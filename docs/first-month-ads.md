## Bid and booked-call strategy

A bid strategy tells Google which outcome to pursue when it enters each eligible search auction. **Maximize Clicks** sets bids to obtain as many clicks as it can within the budget; the entered USD 10 CPC limit prevents Google from bidding above that amount but can reduce reach. It does not distinguish a future $10,000 client from an inexpensive, low-intent visitor. [Google Maximize Clicks guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6336101?hl=en).

Use the current Maximize Clicks setting only as the first controlled demand-validation stage. Review the first USD 50–100 for relevant search terms, valid form submissions and booked calls. Do not launch even that stage until the form-success conversion and lead routing work. When the account can reliably record a booked or qualified call, switch the campaign to **Maximize Conversions without a target CPA** so auction bids pursue that primary action. Maximize Conversions attempts to spend the available budget and therefore does not replace the required hard total budget and end date. [Google Maximize Conversions guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7381968/about-maximize-conversions-bidding).

Do not set Target CPA from an invented benchmark. Adopt it only after SKOX has enough recent, accurately tracked conversion data to estimate a sustainable cost per qualified call. Maximize Conversion Value or Target ROAS belongs later, after qualified leads and closed projects have reliable values. Google recommends qualified or converted leads for offline lead goals and supports importing hashed lead data and GCLIDs through enhanced conversions for leads. [Google enhanced conversions for leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15713840?hl=en_us_us), [offline lead-goal guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10029210?hl=en).

Recommended measurement sequence:

1. Form submitted: record for funnel diagnosis; use as the temporary primary action only until the booking flow is active.
2. Call booked: make this the primary online conversion when a real calendar URL is connected and tested.
3. Qualified opportunity: import after a founder confirms authority, business need, timing and budget fit.
4. Won project and value: import when a signed project closes; use this later for value-based bidding.

The user selected manual follow-up: visitors explain the project and complete the qualification fields at `/web-apps#scope`, then Reiss reviews the details and replies within one business day to arrange the call. Do not represent the flow as instant booking.
