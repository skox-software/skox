## 3. Where SKOX can win

1. **Nobody combines trade-specific and custom.** Subscription companies are trade-specific but off-the-shelf. Studios are custom but generic. A page like "custom software for landscaping companies" has no direct competitor in the ads.
2. **The "own it, don't rent it" angle is proven** (NetKinetix has run it 6+ months) and it fits our trade buyers, who already pay monthly per-seat fees.
3. **A free, specific first step beats a form.** Every long runner offers one: a demo, trial, guide or "free scope & quote in 48 hours".
4. **Uncontested intent exists** (Oct 5 research): "custom software for service business", "custom quoting software for contractors" and "HVAC KPI dashboard" had no ads at all.

## 4. Recommendations

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop bidding on bare "{trade} software" (or keep exact match only). Add "custom" and "own" variants: "custom landscaping software", "custom software for landscaping company", "custom roofing software", "custom crm for contractors", "software we own" | Leaves the subscription auction and targets the searches no one answers |
| 2 | Negatives on the landscaping group: design, 3d, garden, planner, "landscape design", "for homeowners" (in the list), free | "landscaping software" is mostly design-tool and homeowner intent |
| 3 | New ad and page angle: "Own your software. Stop renting it." Built once, you own the code, built around how you work | Proven by NetKinetix. "You own the code" is approved (C07). "No monthly per-seat fees" is a **new claim, so Josh needs to OK it** |
| 4 | New first step: an "Own vs. rent: your 5-year cost" calculator or one-page guide, gated by email like the scorecard | Mirrors the longest-running studio ad; gives visitors who aren't ready to talk something to take |
| 5 | Add "Free scope & price within 48 hours" style speed wording if true (we say "reply within one business day", C10) | Shinetech has run this for 3+ years |
| 6 | Don't lead with "Dream it, we build it" | AI builders own it, and it attracts DIY founders, not owners with a budget |
