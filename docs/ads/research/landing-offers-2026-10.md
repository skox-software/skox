# Competitor landing pages and offers: teardown

Researched 2026-10-05. Ten pages in three groups, plus help-centre docs to check what the field-service tools actually do for multi-location owners. This is research, not ad copy. Nothing here is an approved claim; ad lines still map to `claims.md`. Competitor names appear here only so we know who's who. They never go in SKOX copy (copy-rules #5).

Method: WebSearch + WebFetch. Apify was unavailable (monthly hard limit already exceeded, $0 spent), so one JS-rendered page (Blue Collar Metrics) couldn't be read and is left out. Fetched pages were summarised by a model, so treat the quotes as close paraphrases until someone checks them on the live page. Prices and stats are what each page *claims*; we didn't verify them.

## Group 1: dashboard / BI agencies and productised dashboard services

### 1. Oryx Horn: BI dashboards for HVAC
- **URL:** https://oryx-horn.com/services/business-intelligence-dashboards-for-hvac/
- **Headline:** "Business Intelligence Dashboards for HVAC"
- **Offer:** Custom build, no packages, no price. Timeline stated: diagnostic to live dashboard in "4–6 wks". Sells six named dashboards: revenue, job costing, customer acquisition, technician performance, booking/call centre, custom KPIs. Names the source systems (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, FieldPulse, QuickBooks, spreadsheets, call logs). Qualifies the buyer: "$1M–$15M in annual revenue".
- **Proof:** One industry stat (unsourced on the page). No case studies, no logos, no screenshots. Has a comparison table: custom vs off-the-shelf.
- **Lead capture:** "Get a Free Data Diagnostic", which is a 30-min call plus a data audit. Form: name, email, question. Calendly. A named founder "replies personally within one business day".
- **Strong:** Nearest match to SKOX's dashboard offer and positioning, and it has no proof either, so it shows you can sell without it. The free diagnostic gives the call a name, a reason to book. It lists the six views by name and names the tools the buyer already pays for. **Gap:** nothing on multi-location, and nothing on ad spend to booked jobs beyond "customer acquisition".

### 2. Autymate: multi-unit operators
- **URL:** https://www.autymate.com/solutions/multi-unit-operators
- **Headline:** "Automated Financial Reporting for Multi-Unit Franchise Operators"
- **Offer:** SaaS. No price; it "depends on store count, connected systems". Claims "1 day" to connect all stores. Consolidated P&L, same-store benchmarking, "profit leakage" by store, role-based access for regional managers.
- **Proof:** Heavy. Customer count, a per-store labour saving, hours saved, 300+ integrations, franchise brand logos, a named 15-store operator case study, a franchisee testimonial, product screenshots.
- **Lead capture:** "Book My Free Demo". Form: name, work email, session length (30/45/60), **store count** (1–10 … 500+).
- **Strong:** Speaks straight to the multi-unit owner ("several stores across different systems"). The store-count dropdown qualifies leads and makes the visitor feel the product is sized for them. **Gap:** finance-first (QuickBooks/POS/payroll). Field-service ops metrics like jobs, close rate and technician output, and ad spend to booked jobs, are thin. It's a platform you rent, not something you own.

### 3. Vidi Corp: Power BI consultants
- **URL:** https://vidi-corp.com/consultancy/microsoft-power-bi-consulting-support-services/
- **Headline:** "Expert Power BI Consultants For Powerful Analytics & Automation"
- **Offer:** Price *framework* rather than packages: an entry point around £750 for a simple dashboard, up to £50k for year-long work, plus an hourly rate. Timeline: about a week for minimal dashboards, 1–3 months for full projects.
- **Proof:** Maximal. Superlative ranking, project and client counts, enterprise logos, quantified testimonials, dashboard template screenshots.
- **Lead capture:** "Hire Power BI Consultants". Short form (service dropdown, email). Lead magnet: **a free dashboard** once you connect a data source. Phone, WhatsApp, Telegram.
- **Strong:** The free starter dashboard is the boldest lead magnet in the set. **Weak for our buyer:** enterprise logos and hourly billing say "not for a 6-location HVAC owner". This is the "big shop, billed by the hour" alternative SKOX already positions against (C13).

### 4. Dashboard Doctor: Looker Studio dashboards
- **URL:** https://dashboarddr.com/
- **Headline:** "Your Data, Beautifully Clear."
- **Offer:** No packages shown on the homepage. Shows a "48hr Avg. Turnaround" and a 4-step process (Discovery → Connection → Design → Handoff). Its content pages say simple dashboards go live in 3–5 business days and fixed-price work runs 1–3 weeks.
- **Proof:** Dashboard count, founder's years of experience, three named testimonials, and **three live, clickable sample dashboards** with realistic numbers.
- **Lead capture:** "Start Your Dashboard". Form: name, email, company/website, service dropdown, description. Promises a reply "within one business day". "Satisfaction guaranteed".
- **Strong:** The live sample dashboards are the best proof device for a dashboard seller: the visitor clicks around a real one. SKOX can copy this honestly with SAMPLE data. Also uses city/state SEO pages at scale. SKOX copy can't use location, but targeting can.

### 5. Studio Dahu: custom dashboards for SMEs (article-as-landing-page)
- **URL:** https://studiodahu.ch/en/blog/custom-dashboard-development-sme
- **Headline:** "Custom business dashboard development for SMEs & CEOs"
- **Offer:** A budget range over three years and "6 to 12 weeks" to first deployment. Phases: Immersion → Co-design → Development. ROI framed as hours of manual reporting saved.
- **Proof:** Worked examples by industry (construction, law firm), a founder byline, references to its portfolio.
- **Lead capture:** "Estimate my dashboard project" (twice), newsletter, a FAQ that handles objections ("our data isn't clean", timeline, mobile).
- **Strong:** A long guide that sells. It frames off-the-shelf tools as "the tyranny of standardized templates" and builds a role-based KPI example (owner vs ops manager). The "my data is messy" FAQ is an objection SKOX's buyers will have too.

## Group 2: productised studios with fixed price or subscription

### 6. Designjoy: design subscription
- **URL:** https://www.designjoy.co/
- **Headline:** "Design subscriptions for everyone"
- **Offer:** One plan at a stated monthly price ($4,995/mo, "regular" price struck through), one request at a time, around 48-hour turnaround per request, "Pause or cancel anytime". Week-long trial with a partial refund.
- **Proof:** Brand logos (shown twice), a celebrity investor quote, product awards, a solo founder since 2017.
- **Lead capture:** "Join today" (buy now from the page), "Book a call" secondary, a plain email address.
- **Strong:** Everything is on one card, so there's nothing left to ask. The founder is the brand. **Not for SKOX as-is:** a subscription and a published price both break current rules ("Not a price", "Not retainers"). Also its "until you're 100% satisfied" language is a guarantee we can't use.

### 7. Shipkit: fixed-price MVP studio
- **URL:** https://shipkit.us/
- **Headline:** "Fixed-Price MVP Development for Non-Technical Founders"
- **Offer:** Three packages, each with a price and a week count ($8.9k / 6 wks, $14.9k / 6 wks, $24.9k / 9 wks). Milestone payments 30/30/30/10. "100% code ownership".
- **Proof:** Product-launch count, six named testimonials, client logos, case-study thumbnails, founder bio.
- **Lead capture:** "Book a Free Founder Call", "Get Free Estimate" (an estimator page), phone and email.
- **Strong:** The nearest model to SKOX's structure: fixed price, fixed timeline, own the code, talk to the person building it ("You talk directly to the person building"). Pain-point section about scope-creep invoices and a freelancer who vanished. Weekly Loom demos as a way to lower risk. Milestone payments tied to delivery gates. **Gap:** sold to startup founders, not operators. Nothing about dashboards or service businesses.

### 8. Orbix Studio: dashboard design agency
- **URL:** https://www.orbix.studio/blogs/top-dashboard-design-agencies (blog that ranks itself among "top agencies")
- **Offer (as published):** Fixed-scope dashboard projects at a stated price band, "2–4 weeks for focused features", 6–12 weeks for multi-role products.
- **Strong:** "Focused" scope for the short timeline. It's the same conditional framing SKOX uses for C08 ("14-day *focused* dashboard build"), so the market already accepts that a short date comes with a scope condition. The self-ranking listicle is a common SEO move. SKOX shouldn't copy it, because it amounts to an invented superlative.

## Group 3: field-service software reporting pages (what the buyer already has)

### 9. ServiceTitan: Field Reporting Software (+ Enterprise Hub)
- **URLs:** https://www.servicetitan.com/features/field-reporting-software · https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/enterprise-hub · help docs: https://help.servicetitan.com/v1/docs/rollup-dashboards
- **Headline:** "Field Reporting Software"; sub: "More than just numbers"
- **Promises:** Custom dashboards, revenue by day/week/month, filter by business unit, technician and CSR scorecards, "hundreds of KPIs". Marketing Pro adds first- and last-touch attribution by campaign. **Multi-location:** Enterprise Hub roll-up dashboards across tenants. Its own blog says the sweet spot starts at 2–3 tenants and that one tenant with several business units "doesn't yet need" it.
- **Proof:** Review ratings, contractor count, two revenue-growth case-study numbers, a technician quote.
- **Lead capture:** "Get Demo". Form: industry, technician count, office staff, residential/commercial, job type. No price.
- **Gap:** Roll-up only covers ServiceTitan tenants, and Enterprise Hub is an enterprise upsell. Nothing for an owner whose locations run different systems (an acquired shop on Housecall Pro), whose brands differ, or who wants ad spend (Google/Meta) set against booked jobs and labour across locations unless every piece runs inside ServiceTitan's paid add-ons.

### 10. Housecall Pro: Field Service Reporting (+ franchise page)
- **URLs:** https://www.housecallpro.com/features/advanced-reporting/ · https://www.housecallpro.com/partner/franchise/ · help: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5845648-how-to-use-your-multi-location-hcp-account
- **Headline:** "Field Service Reporting Software with Real-Time Analytics"
- **Promises:** Business Insights with goals and industry benchmarks, job and invoice reports filterable by lead source, technician performance, an AI report builder that answers plain questions, a reporting dashboard. **Multi-location:** a "franchisor mode" with child locations, aggregated job and estimate reports, location filters, and KPI summaries emailed daily or weekly. The franchise page is written for the *franchisor* (brand HQ), not the franchisee who owns several units.
- **Proof:** User count, jobs completed, an average revenue-growth figure, a customer quote, review badges, screenshots.
- **Lead capture:** "Start Free Trial" with no card. "Book a demo" asks first and last name, phone, email, company, and offers a **$50 gift card** for booking. Plans are listed at $59 / $149 / $299 per month on the pricing page. Which reporting features sit on which plan isn't spelled out.
- **Gap:** Same as above: reporting stops at the edge of the platform. Aggregation is built for franchisors, not a multi-unit franchisee. Labour cost and ad spend from outside systems aren't part of it.

### (Supporting) Jobber: Financial management and reporting
- **URL:** https://www.getjobber.com/features/reporting/ · help: https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/115009616247-Franchising-Adding-Another-Account
- **Headline:** "Financial management and reporting tools for field service teams"
- **Promises:** Financial reports, aged receivables, expense tracking, QuickBooks sync, an AI custom report builder. **Multi-location:** Jobber recommends a separate account per location. Linked "franchise" accounts can export **one combined CSV**, and data isn't shared between accounts. The newer "Jobber Central" adds a group view of performance and lead funnel.
- **Proof:** G2 rating, user count, one customer revenue story. CTA "Start Free Trial", no card.
- **Gap:** The buyer's cross-location view is often still a CSV export rebuilt in a spreadsheet every week. That's exactly SKOX's C22 line, "Stop rebuilding the same report".

## Synthesis

### (a) The gap SKOX can own
What the field-service tools give a multi-location owner: good single-location reports, plus a roll-up **only if** every location is on the same platform, usually on an enterprise tier or in franchisor mode. What none of them promise on their landing pages:
1. **One view across systems.** Locations on different software (an acquisition, a second brand, a territory on spreadsheets), joined with QuickBooks, payroll and the ad platforms.
2. **Ad spend → booked jobs → revenue by location.** The software counts lead source. Google and Meta hold the spend. Nobody shows cost per *booked job* per location in one place.
3. **Labour against revenue by location/crew.** Payroll or timesheets sit outside the field-service tool's reports.
4. **Built for the multi-unit franchisee, not the franchisor.** Housecall Pro and the franchise SaaS vendors sell to HQ. The owner of 4–12 units of someone else's brand has nobody writing to them.
5. **Owned, not rented.** Every alternative is a platform subscription or an hourly BI shop. "You own the code" (C07) plus a fixed price and date (C03) is unclaimed in this niche.

How to say it in SKOX copy, already allowlisted: "connected data from your existing tools", "location & team performance views" (C21), "Stop rebuilding the same report / one clear view of your business" (C22). Never name the platforms in copy (copy-rules #5 and the claims.md forbidden list). The *keywords* can target platform-plus-"reporting" searches only if policy-auditor clears it; claims.md says platform names belong in negative keywords only, so check before using them.

### (b) Offer patterns worth copying
| Pattern | Seen at | SKOX version (within rules) |
|---|---|---|
| A named free diagnostic as the call's reason | Oryx Horn | The scope call already exists (C04/C20). Give it a concrete output: "you leave with a written scope, price and date" (C19 / formBenefits). A "data audit" would be a new promise. Needs Josh's sign-off before it goes in an ad. |
| Qualify by unit count in the form | Autymate (store-count dropdown), ServiceTitan (tech count) | Add "How many locations / crews?" to the `/dashboards` form. It lets the form branch, and makes the multi-location owner feel seen. (Site change, not ad copy.) |
| Live, clickable sample dashboards | Dashboard Doctor | A SAMPLE multi-location dashboard on `/dashboards` (see c). |
| Packages with a price and week count | Shipkit, Designjoy, Vidi's "from" price | **Blocked today**: offer.md says "Not a price". A "from $X" needs Josh's decision first. Fallback: show the *structure* (what's in a focused build, what pushes it to a custom date) without a number. |
| Milestone payments tied to delivery | Shipkit 30/30/30/10 | SKOX has the halfway working review (C17). If deposit terms are ever published, pay-at-milestones fits that story. Josh's call. |
| Founder replies in one business day | Oryx Horn, Dashboard Doctor | Already C10 + C06. Keep it near the button. |
| Named views listed out | Oryx Horn (6 dashboards) | List the views as *examples of what a build can include*, wording kept inside C21: location performance, team/crew performance, connected data. No outcome numbers. |
| "My data's a mess" objection in FAQ | Studio Dahu | FAQ line: data cleanliness is part of scoping. The timing condition on C08 already says this. |

Patterns to avoid: guarantees ("100% satisfied", "satisfaction guaranteed"), invented or self-awarded rankings, gift cards for demos, "pause anytime" subscriptions (not offered), struck-through "regular" prices (scarcity is switched off).

### (c) Proof SKOX can create without inventing anything
1. **SAMPLE multi-location dashboard.** A clickable demo on `/dashboards` with made-up data, labelled "SAMPLE — illustrative data" on every screen. Example: a 6-location home-services business with revenue, jobs booked, labour % and ad spend → booked jobs by location. The site already has a "demo tab" per route, so build on that. Never show a sample number in an ad as if it were a result.
2. **Recorded build.** A screen recording of the two founders building that sample from source exports (CSV from a field-service tool, an ads export, a payroll export) to a working view, with the halfway review shown. It proves "work with the founders" (C06) and "review working software at the halfway mark" (C17) without a client.
3. **Public-data teardown.** Take a public franchise brand's published unit-economics (an FDD Item 19 or a public filing) and show what a franchisee's one-view dashboard would need to answer. Describe it generically ("a home-services franchise with N units") in any ad. A named brand is fine only in long-form content, and only if Josh approves it.
4. **Process artefacts.** A redacted sample scope document showing the fixed price and date written down (C03, C18). Real format, fake client.
5. **Our own products (C27).** Tender and FocusTap, always "our own products", to show that we ship.

### (d) Lead magnet ideas
- **"Is your report rebuilt every Monday?" checklist.** One page: which numbers a multi-location owner should see daily, and which tool usually holds each one. Gated by email, with a CTA to the scope call.
- **Sample dashboard walkthrough.** A 15-minute recorded or live tour of the SAMPLE dashboard. A lower-commitment step before the 30-minute scope call.
- **Locations scorecard template.** A spreadsheet that shows the target view (revenue, jobs, labour %, cost per booked job, by location). It hands the owner the problem we solve: they see how much manual work the spreadsheet takes.
- **"What your field-service software's reports don't join" one-pager.** A generic description of the gap in (a), without naming platforms in ads. A landing-page explainer can mention the platforms descriptively only if policy-auditor clears it.
- **Free scope-call output framed as a deliverable.** "Leave with a written scope, price and date. Keep it whether you hire us or not" (C19). It's the strongest magnet we already have, and it's on the allowlist.

### Open decisions for Josh
1. Publish a "from $X" price on `/dashboards`? Every studio with a fixed-price story (Shipkit, Designjoy, Vidi's entry price) shows a number. SKOX's rules forbid it today.
2. Name the scope call "free data audit/diagnostic"? It's stronger, but it's a new promise (a real audit of their data) that needs a defined deliverable before it goes in an ad.
3. Add a location/crew-count field to the dashboard form?
4. Approve building the SAMPLE multi-location dashboard and the recorded build as the first proof assets.
