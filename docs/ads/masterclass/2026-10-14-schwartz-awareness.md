# Awareness and sophistication: SKOX offer review (2026-10-14)
Framework: *Breakthrough Advertising*, Eugene Schwartz, 1966. Elements used: the five levels of prospect awareness (Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, Most Aware); the five stages of market sophistication (1 direct claim, 2 enlarge the claim, 3 new mechanism, 4 elaborate the mechanism, 5 identification); channeling mass desire instead of creating it.

Scope: the trade landing pages (`content/trades.ts`, `app/for/[trade]/page.tsx`) and the live Google Search offer (`docs/ads/campaigns/google-search-v2-web-upload/5-responsive-search-ads.csv`, keywords in `docs/ads/campaigns/google-search-v2.md` §4 and `docs/ads/reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:47`). Data caveat (README rule 6): 107 impressions, 10 clicks and 0 leads in total (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:78-81`). Nothing below is a proven pattern. These are framework judgements.

## Verdict in one paragraph

SKOX is bidding in two different awareness situations with one message. The original long-tail trade keywords ("hvac kpi dashboard", "plumbing dispatch software", "electrician truck stock app") are **Solution Aware** searches, and the ads answer them correctly: the pinned headline 1 names the solution in the searcher's words (`5-responsive-search-ads.csv:2-9`, Headline 1). The trouble is volume. Almost nobody types those phrases, and 27 of 93 keywords can't serve at all (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:24`). The "{trade} software" keywords added on Oct 11 (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:47`) are a different case. Those searchers are Solution Aware of *subscription* software and often already Product Aware of specific vendors. Their market is at **sophistication stage 4–5**: years-old ads, "#1" claims, social proof and free trials (`research/competitors-2026-10-14.md:25-36`). Against that, SKOX's hero ("Custom landscaping software. Built at speed.", `trades.ts:158`, `page.tsx:88-89`) is a stage-1 direct claim plus a speed claim. Speed is a weak promise to someone who can start a free trial today. What can still break through is a **new mechanism** for that market: software built around how *your* company runs, which you own instead of rent. The repo already holds most of the words (`trades.ts:163`, `page.tsx:147`, `landing.ts:134`), but no headline or hero states the contrast. In the *studio* market, SKOX's trade specificity is already the right stage-5 move. It identifies with the prospect ("For Roofing Contractors", pinned at position 2, `reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:71`). Keep that move and lean into it.

## What not to change, and why

- **The pinned H1 = the searcher's own phrase** (`5-responsive-search-ads.csv:2-9`). For a Solution Aware searcher the headline should lead with the solution they named, and it does. Rewrites should add a mechanism, not remove the echo.
- **The pinned trade qualifier at position 2** (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:71`). It's the stage-5 identification move and screens out homeowners. Josh applied it; note it's not yet listed in `claims.md` (see open questions).
- **The "Sound familiar?" pains section** (`page.tsx:113-124`). It catches the Problem Aware reader who scrolls, and the pains feed T02 headlines. Keep it on the page. The change in #1 only reduces how many of them compete for the single rotating ad slot.
- **The concept-labelled demo** (`page.tsx:141-142`). It's the closest thing SKOX has to the "free first step" that long-running competitors offer (`competitors-2026-10-14.md:36, 57`), and it's honest under the no-invented-proof rule.
- **The certainty tail (C03/C05/C07/C04/C11).** In a stage-4 studio market these mechanisms are shared, so they don't win the click on their own. They're still exactly what a Product Aware buyer needs before acting, so they stay as support lines.
- **No proof-based identification.** Competitors identify through "#1" and "Most Recommended" (`competitors-2026-10-14.md:33-34`). SKOX has no proof (`claims.md:60`), so the stage-5 route has to be trade identification, not social proof. The way to earn proof later is a first delivered trade build with the client's written OK to name it. Until then, don't display any.
