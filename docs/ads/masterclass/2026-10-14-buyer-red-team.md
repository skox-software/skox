# Buyer red team — SKOX offer review (2026-10-14)
Framework: composite buyer objection review (the panel's red-team method in `.claude/agents/mc-buyer-red-team.md`; no published author framework is applied, so no author is cited). Elements used: the objection hierarchy price → risk → time → switching → trust → fit, plus other objections found, read screen by screen in render order.

Persona: the owner of a 10–40 person landscaping or roofing company, reading on a phone between jobs, who already pays for a field-service subscription and has been burned by a vendor before. This is a composite, not a real person.

Scope: Landscaping and Roofing RSAs (`docs/ads/campaigns/google-search-v2-web-upload/5-responsive-search-ads.csv:5`, `:6`), `/for/landscaping` and `/for/roofing` (`app/for/[trade]/page.tsx` + `content/trades.ts:152–209`), shared copy (`content/landing.ts`), the form (`components/ConversionForm.tsx`). Data caveat: 10 paid clicks, 0 leads, 7 Clarity recordings (`docs/ads/reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:76–117`). Nothing below is presented as a proven pattern. The recordings are only used to check that a guessed objection matches what one visitor did.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The page answers "what is it?" well and "can I trust you with money?" well (fixed price, a date in writing, you own the code). It does not answer the first three questions this owner asks: **what does it cost, what does it cost every month after, and why would I build this when I already pay for software that does most of it?** On a trade page the cost FAQ, the after-launch FAQ, the change FAQ and the founders section are all missing, even though the homepage has approved copy for every one (`landing.ts:310–329`, `:219–236`). The landscaping visit is weakest: the owner searched "landscaping software", the ad still sells mower engine health and lands on a `/fleet` path, and the "working example" invites him to "change its color and labels in the inspector" (`trades.ts:167`). That reads as a dashboard toy, not as software to run his company. Roofing is tighter: the problem is real and the demo does the math. But it has no proof that anyone has built one for a roofer, and the page honestly says so. That honesty is correct, and it is still the single biggest trust gap. Most fixes reuse copy that is already approved. Two need Josh: the subscription comparison line, and what "keep your current software" actually means.

## What not to change, and why
- **The concept disclaimer** (`page.tsx:142`, "A concept with invented sample data, not a client's system"). It costs some trust, but a burned owner who later found out a "working example" was fake would be lost for good. It's also required (`claims.md:83–84`).
- **The click-to-call line and the sticky Call button** (`page.tsx:101–103`, `TradeStickyBar.tsx:45`). For a phone-first owner between jobs, a number is the lowest-friction step on the page, and the strongest "real people" signal there is.
- **The roofing page's structure and pains** (`trades.ts:196–205`). This is the best ad-to-page match in the set, written in the owner's own words. The deposit wording at `:203` is fine once change #4 makes the scope-call line say "for the call".
- **Fixed price + date in writing + you own the code** (`landing.ts:132–134`, `trades.ts:62–65`). This answers the burned-before buyer directly. Keep it in the hero.
- **No price on the page.** Rule 5 and `claims.md:63` forbid it. Change #1 explains *how* pricing works without a number.
- **CONNECT_FAQ** (`trades.ts:58–61`). Its "usually yes, confirmed before you pay" is the honest answer. Don't strengthen it to "works with everything".

## Open questions for Josh (only facts the repo can't answer)
1. **Keep vs replace:** for landscaping and roofing, is the build meant to sit beside the owner's current field-service software, or can it replace it? This decides whether the change #2 line is true.
2. **Owner time:** roughly how many hours of the owner's (or office manager's) time does a typical build need: scope call, data access, halfway review, launch? That's the "I don't have time" answer. UNKNOWN today.
3. **Running cost:** is there a typical monthly hosting/support figure you'd let the page *describe* (not price), e.g. "hosting is billed at cost" or "support is optional"? `landing.ts:312`, `:328` say it's separate, but not what it is.
4. **Hardware:** does the landscaping fleet build assume telematics already on the trucks and mowers? If yes, the page should say so. If no, say how engine hours get in.
5. **Phone coverage:** who answers (904) 792-0720 and when (`content/site.ts:17`)? An unanswered call from this buyer is worse than no number.
6. **First trade pilot:** would you trade anything (scope, timing, a smaller first version) for a named, quotable first trade client? That's the only legitimate route to the proof this page is missing.
