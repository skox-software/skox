# Paid search — SKOX offer review (2026-10-14)
Framework: "Ultimate Guide to Google Ads" (Perry Marshall, Mike Rhodes & Bryan Todd, 6th ed., 2020) and "80/20 Sales and Marketing" (Perry Marshall, 2013), applied as published themes. Elements used: **Message-to-market match**, **Tight relevance (narrow ad groups + negatives)**, **Testing**, **80/20 concentration**, plus the direct-response rule that **a click is worth (value of a lead × conversion rate)**, which sets the max CPC.

Scope: the eight `/for/` trade pages and the live Google Search campaign. Read-only; no account, site or file changes were made.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The campaign is spread across 9 live ad groups and ~110 keywords on $9.80/day (`docs/ads/campaigns/google-search-v2.md:23`, `docs/ads/reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:46-47`), yet all 10 paid clicks in week one came from two keywords: a homeowner term that is now paused and "landscaping software" (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:84`). That's the 80/20 pattern before it's been asked for, but at 10 clicks it isn't proof of anything (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:87`). The biggest weakness is **message-to-market match**. The bare "{trade} software" keywords added on Oct 11 put SKOX into subscription-software auctions it can't win at a $4 cap (`research/competitors-2026-10-14.md:23`). The searchers in those auctions want a monthly product with a free trial, and they land on pages selling one custom tool, such as "HVAC business dashboards" (`content/trades.ts:72`). The Oct 14 landscaping rewrite fixed the H1 (`content/trades.ts:158`), but the ad's display path, most of its keywords and the live demo still say "fleet" (`docs/ads/campaigns/google-search-v2-web-upload/5-responsive-search-ads.csv:5`, `3-keywords.csv:24-31`, `content/trades.ts:165-166`). My recommendation: concentrate the $300 on four tight ad groups built on uncontested or "custom"-qualified intent. Bid by keyword, not with a blanket $7 cap. Measure micro-conversions, because A/B testing isn't possible at this volume. Treat Google as a capped intent probe, not the main lead channel, until Josh fills in the route prices that set a cost-per-lead ceiling (`docs/ads/economics.md:20-22`).

## What not to change, and why

- **The position-2 "For {Trade} Companies" qualifier** (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:71`). It's the cleanest message-to-market filter in the account. It tells homeowners and subscription browsers who the ad is for before they pay for a click.
- **Keyword = pinned H1 = page H1** on Roofing, Plumbing, Garage doors and Electrical (`content/trades.ts:186,214,242,270`; `google-search-v2.md:280,303,326,351`). This is textbook tight relevance. Don't loosen it with generic headlines.
- **The cross-trade ad-group negatives** (`google-search-v2.md:173`). They keep each trade's searches on its own page.
- **Search partners, Display, broad match and AI Max all off** (`google-search-v2.md:95-99`). At 10 clicks a week, any automated expansion would drown the signal.
- **Services stays paused** (`reviews/2026-10-11-google.md:23,46`). Every keyword was below first-page bid at $3. Don't restart it at this budget.
- **No price, proof or competitor names in ads**, even though the subscription advertisers lead with "#1" and free trials (`research/competitors-2026-10-14.md:34-36`; `claims.md:60,63,65,67`). Being the custom, owned option is the difference that makes SKOX stand out. Copying their proof claims isn't possible honestly.
- **The 14-day promise stays off trade ads** (`google-search-v2.md:143`).
