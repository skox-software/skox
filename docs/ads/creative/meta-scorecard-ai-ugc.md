# Creative brief: AI presenter video for the free scorecard (Meta prospecting)

Status: draft 2026-10-05. Not generated. Must pass claims-auditor and policy-auditor, and Reiss signs the new claims, before any credits are spent or any ad runs.

## The formula we're borrowing

From the research (`docs/ads/research/playbook-2026-10.md`):
- **Real-person, on-the-job feel** (Jobber's operator partnership ad, ~146 days, 6+ copies).
- **Pain-moment hook** ("opening five tabs", "nights and weekends on paperwork").
- **Free template as the offer** (Jobber and ServiceTitan templates, the longest-running ads in the market).

## The honesty line

- The presenter is a **presenter**, talking to owners: "If you run three locations…".
- The presenter **never** says or implies "I own a business", "I use this", "my locations", or any result.
- No invented reviews, ratings, customer names or numbers. Sample data stays labelled SAMPLE.
- Screens are **real**: screenshots of the actual scorecard workbook. Nothing generated or restyled.
- Disclose AI in the ad where Meta offers the "AI info" label, and keep "Made with AI" off the hook so it doesn't hurt the first second.

## Format (Higgsfield UGC "website" format)

- 9:16, 15 seconds, one presenter on camera speaking continuously, phone-camera look, no music.
- 3–4 real screenshot cards overlaid at word cues: the Scorecard tab, the Watch column, the Trend chart, the Start here page.
- Captions on. Keep text out of the bottom ~35% for Reels (Higgsfield's default keeps only 15% clear; adjust in assembly).
- The presenter is generated, or, better, built from a photo of Josh with his consent so it's really him without filming. If it's Josh, say so in the copy: "I'm Josh, I co-founded SKOX".

## Script (about 37 words, 15s)

| Beat | Words | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Hook, 0–2s | "Running three locations? Monday shouldn't mean five logins." | Presenter only |
| Body | "This free scorecard puts every location side by side: revenue, jobs, labour, ad spend." | Scorecard tab card |
| Body | "And it flags who's slipping." | Watch column card, then Trend chart card |
| Closer | "Grab it free at skoxsoftware.com. Ten minutes a week." | Presenter only |

Alternate hooks to test, one per variant (one bet each):
1. **Visibility:** "Running three locations? Monday shouldn't mean five logins."
2. **Slipping:** "One of your locations is slipping. Can you tell which?"
3. **Spreadsheet:** "Still rebuilding the same report every Monday?"
