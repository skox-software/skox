## Why

1. **Services is outbid.** All 15 Services exact-match keywords are "Below first page bid". Google's estimates run $4.37–$37.45, mostly $15–32, against our $3.00 max. At a $3.30/day budget, one click at market price would use more than a full day's budget. With the $300/month cap, this campaign can't compete.
2. **Trade keywords barely get searched.** 27 of 93 keywords are "Low search volume" and can't serve. The 51 eligible ones are rarely searched: people don't type "hvac technician performance dashboard".
3. **The phrase match on "roofing estimating tool"** pulls in homeowner calculator searches.

| | Value |

- **Where the clicks came from:** "roofing estimating tool" 4 (paused Oct 12, homeowners) and **"landscaping software" 6** (57 impr., $22.39). Every other "{trade} software" keyword: 0 impressions in 3 days.
- **Search terms:** Google hides them all except "calculate shingles for roof" (low-volume privacy threshold).
- **GA4:** only ~5 paid sessions recorded for 10 clicks, 1 engaged. Server response is fine (TTFB ~0.3–0.5s), so the gap is instant bounces, ad blockers and normal GA loss.
- **Statistically:** 0 leads from 10 clicks tells us nothing yet. Even at a 5% lead rate, the chance of 0 in 10 is ~60%. A fair verdict needs ~60–100 clicks.

### Diagnosis
1. **Delivery is capped by the $4.00 CPC limit.** Only half the budget is being spent. The high-volume trade software keywords (HVAC, plumbing, roofing) get no impressions because the off-the-shelf software vendors outbid $4.
2. **Intent mismatch.** "landscaping software" searchers are mostly shopping for off-the-shelf subscriptions. The /for/landscaping page pitches a custom fleet dashboard and doesn't answer "why custom instead of a subscription?".
3. **High-commitment CTA.** The only next step is a full scope form. There's no lighter option (book a call, call the number, try the scorecard) on the trade pages.

### Recommendations (need Josh's OK)
A. Raise the Trades max CPC cap from $4 to $7. The budget stays at $9.80/day; we're only spending ~$5/day, so this buys entry into the bigger auctions, not more spend.
B. Add an "own it, don't rent it" section to /for/* pages, aimed at people comparing subscriptions: one-time build, you own the code, built around how you work. Wording beyond the existing claims (e.g. "no per-seat fees") needs a sign-off first.
C. Add lighter CTAs on /for/*: a "Book a 15-min call" link (calendar) and a click-to-call number, plus the CTA right under the demo.
D. Watch the Microsoft Clarity recordings for paid sessions (filter: utm_source=google). That's the fastest way to see where people drop off.
E. Finish advertiser verification (Billing banner).

- **Not one tap or click in any paid session.** Nobody pressed a CTA, the demo or the "Try the example" button.
- **Nobody reached the form.** On the /for/landscaping desktop scroll map, 75% reached the 70% depth mark and 0% went past 80%. The scope form sits in the last ~20% of the page.
- The desktop visitor read the hero and "Sound familiar?" for about a minute without scrolling to the demo.
- The iPhone visitor spent ~5 active minutes and came back the next morning: real interest, but there was no step they were willing to take.

### What this means
1. **Message mismatch.** People search "landscaping software" (running the business: scheduling, crews, estimates, invoicing), but the page leads with "Landscaping fleet and equipment dashboards". Fleet health is one narrow use case, so most people don't see their problem.
2. **The ask is too far down and too big.** The only conversion is a full form at the very bottom, and nobody gets there.
3. **The demo doesn't pull people in.** On mobile it's below several screens of text.

### Fixes (proposed)
- Rewrite the /for/landscaping hero around running a landscaping business ("Landscaping software built around how your crews work"), with fleet as one example.
- Add a sticky mobile bar: "Get my price & timeline" + "Call". Add an inline CTA under the hero and under the demo.
- Offer a lighter step than the full form: a 15-min call booking or tap-to-call.
- Move the demo higher on mobile.
