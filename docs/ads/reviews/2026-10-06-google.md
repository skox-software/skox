## The four problems

1. **35 of 47 keywords are broad match.** At $500–1,000/month, broad match lets Google show the ad for loosely related searches (students, job seekers, "free app builder", enterprise RFPs). It's the fastest way to spend the test budget on clicks that never enquire. Our plan was phrase and exact only.
2. **The keywords are the most expensive, most crowded searches in the market.** "Custom software development company", "web app development agency", "top custom software development companies": these are bid on by agencies with big budgets and list sites. The research found that the trade-specific searches had **no ads at all**, and we built eight pages for exactly those.
3. **Several keywords sell things SKOX doesn't offer, or attract the wrong buyer.**
4. **One ad group for everything.** Every searcher sees the same ad and lands on the same page, so "custom dashboard development" and "web app developer" get identical treatment. Google rates that as low relevance and charges more per click.

The report also has rows for "AI Max expanded matches" and "URL inclusions". If AI Max for Search is on, Google will also match searches outside your keyword list and can send people to other pages on the site. Check and turn it off (Campaign → Settings → AI Max).

## Settings to check before it serves

- [ ] Networks: Search only. Search partners off, Display off.
- [ ] AI Max for Search: off. Automatically created assets: off.
- [ ] Locations: United States, "Presence: people in or regularly in". Not "interest".
- [ ] Bidding: Maximize clicks with a max CPC cap ($8–10 to start), or Manual CPC. Not Maximize conversions until 15+ conversions in 30 days.
- [ ] Budget: about $20/day total, split across ad groups via separate campaigns if you want the trade groups protected from the expensive generic ones.
- [ ] Conversion: the lead form conversion is set up as "website, set up manually with code", and `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_ADS_ID` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_ADS_LEAD_LABEL` are set in Vercel Production. Without these, Google can't see a single enquiry and will optimize blind. Run the test lead in `docs/ads/tracking.md`.
- [ ] Final URL suffix: `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_content={adgroupid}&utm_term={keyword}`, auto-tagging on.
