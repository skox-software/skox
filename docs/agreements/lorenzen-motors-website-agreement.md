# Website Build Agreement

Between **SKOX Software** ("we") and **Lorenzen Motors** ("you"), represented by Jens Lorenzen.
Date: September 16, 2026

---

**The build.** We'll design and build a website for Lorenzen Motors. It will work on
phones and computers. The pages and features are the ones we agree at kickoff, written
up in the project brief in your client portal so we're both looking at the same list.
Anything on top of that is new work, and we'll quote it for you before we start.

**Price: $1,250.**

- **$625 deposit**, due before we begin.
- **$625 at delivery**, before the site goes live.

**Delivery date: October 1, 2026.** We commit to that date. The one thing that moves
it is waiting on you — if materials or feedback come back late, the date moves by the
same number of days.

**Revisions.** Three rounds of changes are included, any time between now and
delivery. A round is one batch of changes sent together. Beyond three rounds, or new
pages and features, we'll quote separately.

**What you provide:** logos, images, and all the text for the site, plus access to
your EarthLink account. Feedback on each review within **3 business days** — if it
takes longer, the delivery date moves by the extra days.

Everything you send us needs to be yours to use: your photos, your logo, your words,
or material you've licensed. If something turns out to belong to someone else and they
raise it, resolving that — including any costs — sits with you.

**Hosting, and what it means.** You're keeping your EarthLink hosting and your domain,
registered through EarthLink, rather than hosting with us. We'll publish the finished
site to your EarthLink account and hand it over to you.

Because the site won't live with us, **we won't be able to maintain it or report on it
after handover.** No ongoing updates or fixes once the 30 days below are up, no
monitoring, no backups, and no traffic or lead analytics — we have no access to the
site or its data. We're also not responsible for EarthLink's uptime, speed, email or
support, and we'll build to what your plan there supports. If something won't run on
it, we'll tell you before we build it.

If you'd like us to host it later, that's **$59 per month**: hosting, ongoing
maintenance and updates, monitoring and backups, and analytics on your traffic and
leads. You can start any time. Moving the site over is quoted separately, and any new
build work after that is quoted at our standard rates.

**Delivery and final payment.** At delivery we'll send you the finished site to review.
If we don't hear back with changes within **5 business days**, it counts as approved
and the final $625 is due. The site goes live on your host once that payment clears.
If an invoice goes unpaid past 30 days, we can pause the project and hold the work
until it's settled.

**Ownership.** Once the final payment is made, the site is yours: design, code and
content. Until then it stays ours. We may show the finished site in our portfolio.

**After launch.** We'll fix anything broken in what we built, free, for 30 days after
the site goes live. After that, fixes are quoted, or covered by the $59/month plan
above.

**If either of us calls it off.** Either of us can stop the project in writing. The
deposit isn't refunded — it covers the work already done. Nothing further is owed.

**Where our responsibility ends.** We build the site described here and stand behind
that work. What we can be held responsible for is capped at what you've paid us under
this agreement. Things outside the build itself — lost sales, lost data, or anything
caused by EarthLink or another outside service — aren't something we can take on.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Signing electronically
counts the same as signing on paper.
