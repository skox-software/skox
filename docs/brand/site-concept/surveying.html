One address, start to finish.

The sample job above, step by step. Each step is a setting in your rulebook, not a rebuild.

  1. A new address comes in. The desk pulls the appraiser record, plat and deed.

    From the county sites your staff open today. A new county is added once and works for every job after.

  2. It checks the flood zone, zoning and setbacks.

    Reads the FEMA layer and the municipal code and writes down what it found, with the source.

  3. It finds your prior jobs nearby.

    Searches the firm's own folders by distance, so a lot you surveyed last year is priced like one.

  4. The quote arrives as a finished draft, from your rate sheet.

    Your rates, your job types, your wording, written into the quote template the office already sends.

  5. Everything is filed in the job folder. The surveyor approves, changes or sends.

    The records, the notes and the draft, in the folder structure the crew uses. Nothing goes out until a person says so.
