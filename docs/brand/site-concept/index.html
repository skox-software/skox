A desk does the legwork. Your people make the calls.

Every desk has the same four parts, built around how your office actually works.

CollectThe desk

Pulls documents and data in by itself.

County sites, email, uploads, QuickBooks, the software you already run. Nothing is retyped.

ReadThe desk

Reads each document and applies your rules.

A new threshold, county or staff member is a setting you change yourself, not a rebuild.

PrepareThe desk

Every job arrives as a finished draft.

The quote, the filing, the packet or the statement, written and filed, ready for a person to look at.

Hand offYours

Stops at your decision. Every time.

You approve, sign or send. The desk logs it and learns from it. The judgement, the signature and the client stay yours.
