A desk does the legwork. Your people make the calls.

Every desk has the same four parts, built around how your office already works. Three are the desk's. The last one is yours.

01

Collect

Pulls documents and data in by itself: county sites, email, uploads, QuickBooks, the software you already run. Nothing is retyped.

02

Read

Reads each document and applies your rules. A new threshold, county or staff member is a setting you change, not a rebuild.

03

Prepare

Every job arrives as a finished draft: the quote, the filing, the packet or the statement, written and filed.

Yours

Decide

You approve, sign or send. The desk logs it and learns from it. The judgement, the signature and the client stay yours.
