## Watch-outs (not for the page)

- All six 1–2 star reviews are 2006–2020 and most are about booking: cancelled same day, card refused for a check, turned away at check-in, a price that changed on the phone.
- Recurring physical complaints: small showers, blinds not curtains, road noise at the inn.
- Owner names: guests name "Connie" (TripAdvisor through 2026) and the Airbnb host is "Mel". Neither is self-stated as owner on their site. The page's own copy names no one; only the R3 quote says "Mel", as the guest wrote it.
