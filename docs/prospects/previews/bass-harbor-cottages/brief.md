## The owner in one paragraph

Owner-run waterfront lodging on Bass Harbor, the "quietside" of Mount Desert Island, five minutes from Bass Harbor Head Light (L10–L11). They have four places to stay: the Pine Cottage (up to 4 guests), the Carriage House (up to 5, with a sauna), the Boat House suites (2–6) and rooms in an 1890 Country Inn (L20–L34). Each has published weekly and daily "from" rates and minimum stays. The hosts live on site, and on Airbnb they say they have since 1986 (L6, L60). Booking today means a phone call between 8am and 7pm and a deposit by check or money order (L37, L40). The homepage date picker sends guests to a separate availabilityonline.com page to "Request Reservation" (L48). Guests love the view from the porch, being right on the water, the quiet side away from Bar Harbor crowds, and a responsive host (top phrases in reviews.md). On Airbnb they are a Superhost at 4.79 from 371 reviews (L57), and the Pine Cottage scores 4.96 from 26 (L58).

## The one working upgrade: "Plan your stay"

A stay planner that replaces the phone call and the third-party picker, built on their own rules:
- Choose a place (Pine Cottage, Carriage House, Boat House suites, or the Johns Island room or Tapley Suite at the Inn), an arrival date, nights and party size.
- The planner enforces their published minimum stays (3/4/5 nights) and maximum occupancy, children included (L21–L34, L38).
- It shows the published "from" rate when they publish one for that month (May or August). For other months it says "rate confirmed by phone". It never shows a total, and it always says "plus Maine lodging tax; cleaning and security as needed" (L39).

- The guest ticks the house rules: quiet hours 9pm–9am, no pets, smoking, drones or grills, a vehicle needed (L44–L46).

"In the build" cards: card deposit at checkout, live availability, pre-arrival guide sent automatically, one rate table across the site (their pages disagree today, L53).

Every 1–2★ review is from 2006–2020, and most are about booking friction. That's the strongest argument for the working upgrade, but Reiss shouldn't raise it as criticism.

- **HospitalityMaine membership** is self-stated only. It's left off the page (the Chamber listing is verified).
- Photos are 2013–2022 and only 923–1522px wide. They're fine at the sizes we'll use, but there's no truly large hero image. A "we'll shoot it fresh" note isn't needed.
- No Facebook posts since April 2025.
