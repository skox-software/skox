## Programs, in her words

Credit Literacy & Strategy · Comprehensive Credit Evaluation · White Glove
Service · **DTI Utilization Program** (30-60 day asset-backed loan at 15-20% to
cut utilization) · **Foreign & International Credit Literacy** (new SSN or ITIN,
approvable profile in 4-6 months) · **Fast Track to Business Credit** (her book).

Her core teaching is the Five Factors: payment history 35, utilization 30,
history 15, mix 10, new credit 10.

## Compliance posture, from her About page

Credit Snitch LLC positions itself as a B2B marketing and consumer engagement
company delivering permission-based leads to approved partners, CrossCountry
Mortgage named. It states it does not originate loans, underwrite, make credit
decisions or furnish consumer reports.

Everything in the concept respects that: the consumer pulls their own report
through SmartCredit, Readiness is always labelled a planning tool, and the
Readiness Letter carries that disclaimer.
