# How Credit Snitch runs today

Observed 2026-09-20 from creditsnitch.com, csrealtorlenderguide.com,
creditsnitchguide.com, secureclientaccess.com and her published affiliate links.
Everything here is public and published by her.

## Two funnels, two doors

The homepage splits every visitor immediately: **I'm a Realtor / Lender** and
**I'm a Consumer**. They go to different domains and different systems.

### Consumer funnel — creditsnitchguide.com

A free guide in exchange for name, email, phone and an SMS opt-in. Positioning
on this property is "The Leading Corporate Credit Strategist For Capital
Readiness" and the lead magnet is the "Empowerment Playbook."

The guide's six chapters, in her order:

1. What is a credit score?
2. Five credit score factors
3. Roadmap to credit success
4. Restore your credit checklist
5. Lender and Realtor pre-approval
6. Next steps to get guidance from Credit Snitch

Her SMS consent wording, verbatim and reusable: *"I authorize Credit Snitch to
text me real-time game changing credit news and tips to accelerate my credit
readiness. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."*

### Partner funnel — csrealtorlenderguide.com

Titled "Unqualified Buyer's Pipeline Onboarding." Form captures name, email,
mobile, with SMS reminders 15 minutes before a webinar. Three steps:

1. **Download the Lender/Realtor guide.** A PDF describing services.
2. **Download the client intro email template.** The partner copies and pastes
   it into their own email and copies asoto@creditsnitch.com.
3. **"Automated client consultation."** The intro sends the client to a
   video-guided signup site. Once a client enrols, the partner *"will
   automatically receive a monthly progress report calendar invite."*

Copyright on this page reads 2017, but the domain was registered in July 2024
and Credit Snitch LLC was formed in September 2023. The copyright line is
inaccurate; the funnel itself is recent.

cases."

## Where the manual work is

Five places, in order of how much the app changes them.

**1. The monthly progress report is a calendar invite.** Her partner funnel
promises a "monthly progress report calendar invite," which means the report
itself is produced by a person on a recurring schedule, per partner, per month.
This is also the single thing partners praise most: *"The monthly updates ensure
I always know where my clients stand"* (Mark R., Century 21 BE3). Highest
labour, highest praise, entirely manual.

**2. Partner onboarding is copy and paste.** Step two literally asks the partner
to paste a template into their own email client. A referral link and QR code
replaces steps one and two outright.

**3. Her brand disappears at the point of highest engagement.** The client logs
in to a generic, unbranded, password-protected portal belonging to another
company. Every day a client checks their progress, they see someone else's
software.

**4. Nothing shows a client where they stand between updates.** Any client
asking "where am I, what do I do next" is a phone call or an email to a
four-person team.

**5. Four affiliate links depend on someone remembering to send them.**

## Things to check with her, not assume

- **Her conversion figure is published two ways.** creditsnitch.com says "over
  $27 million in unqualified buyers converted into successful homeowners."
  csrealtorlenderguide.com says "over 24 million in converted sales." Ask which
  is current before either number is used anywhere.
- The Latino division is called **Credit Snitch Latino** on the team page and
  **Credit Snitch Español** on the partner funnel. Two names, one division.
- **Connect X Network** has its own top-level nav item, separate from
  Partnership, which supports treating it as distinct from the referral-partner
  brand.
- A published client testimonial describes going "from low 500's to the 700
  club" in three months. That is a score-improvement claim and belongs in the
  CROA conversation with her counsel.
- The partner funnel page carries a 2017 copyright on a domain registered in
  2024, for an LLC formed in 2023. That claims a tenure the public record does
  not support. The guide site also has a typo in its own headline ("Coporate").
