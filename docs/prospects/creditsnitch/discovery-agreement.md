Date: _____________

---

**What this is.** Before anyone commits to building Credit Snitch, three things
have to be known: what your credit data provider will actually give us, what
"Qualified" means in your own words for each goal, and what Snitchy has to learn
from. Discovery answers all three and ends with a fixed price and a fixed date
for the build. It is two weeks of real work, not a sales call.

**Price: $4,500.** Due in full before we begin.

**It comes back to you.** If you sign the Phase One build agreement within 60
days of delivery, the entire $4,500 is credited against it. Discovery costs you
nothing if you proceed.

---

## What you get

**1. A credit data feasibility report.** The product depends entirely on reading
a consumer's credit report on demand. We will establish, in writing, from
ConsumerDirect (SmartCredit) and at least one alternative provider:

- Whether we can receive structured account-level data through an API, or only
  a display widget. This single answer decides whether Credit Snitch can be
  built as designed.
- Tri-bureau or single bureau, and which scores.
- What it costs, per enrolled member or per refresh, and how often data refreshes.
- Who owns the identity verification step and how much of it we can brand.
- Whether we are permitted to read data per session and store none of it. This
  decides whether "we never keep your report" survives the contract.
- Whether a sandbox exists to build against.
- What your existing SmartCredit affiliate residual is worth against the data
  cost per member, which may make the data layer self-funding.

**2. Your Rulebook, filled in.** A working session per goal where you set the
thresholds: score floor, unpaid collections, late payments in twelve months,
utilization ceiling, debt-to-income ceiling, minimum file age. This is the
document the engine runs on. It is your judgement, written down once and applied
to every client the same way.

**3. A Snitchy source inventory.** What teaching material exists today, where it
lives, what format it is in, and what is missing. Snitchy can only answer from
your material, so this list is the boundary of what it will know on day one.

**4. An integration inventory.** What your GoHighLevel, Credit Repair Cloud,
Calendly and payment accounts actually permit through their APIs, and what has
to change.

**5. A compliance question list for your attorney.** The specific questions your
counsel needs to answer before launch, written so they can be answered in one
sitting rather than researched from scratch. It covers the partner portal, the
Readiness Letter, and dispute handling. We write the questions. Your attorney
writes the answers.

**6. A Phase One build agreement** with a fixed price, a fixed scope and a fixed
delivery date.

## What we need from you

- An introduction to your ConsumerDirect account representative, and permission
  for us to ask them technical questions on your behalf.
- Read access to your GoHighLevel account, or a working session with whoever
  administers it.
- Your existing program materials, guides and any written SOPs.
- About four hours of your time across three working sessions, scheduled in the
  first week.

Everything you send us needs to be yours to use. If material turns out to belong
to someone else and they raise it, resolving that sits with you.

**Timing: two weeks** from the day we have the access above. If access or
feedback takes longer, the delivery date moves by the same number of days.

---

## The rest of it

**What discovery is not.** We are not writing production code, designing new
screens, or changing the concept you have already seen. If discovery shows the
product should be built differently, we will say so and explain why, and the
Phase One agreement will reflect it.

**Honest findings.** If discovery finds that no provider will give us what the
product needs at a price that works, we will tell you plainly and recommend
against the build. You will still have the Rulebook, the source inventory, the
integration inventory and the question list, and you will have spent $4,500
instead of a great deal more.

**Ownership.** Everything discovery produces is yours on payment.

**Confidentiality.** Neither of us discloses the other's non-public business
information. We may describe the engagement in general terms in our portfolio
and will not publish your thresholds, materials or client information.

**Legal and compliance.** We are software developers. We are not attorneys and
we do not give legal, tax or compliance advice. The question list we produce is
for your counsel, not a substitute for them.

**If either of us calls it off.** Either of us can stop in writing. Fees for
work already done are not refunded. Nothing further is owed.

**Where our responsibility ends.** We do the work described here and stand
behind it. What we can be held responsible for is capped at what you have paid
us under this agreement. Lost business, lost data, or anything caused by an
outside service is not something we can take on.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Signing
electronically counts the same as signing on paper.
