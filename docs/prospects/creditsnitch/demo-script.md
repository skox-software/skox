# Credit Snitch demo — 12 minutes

**Live:** https://creditsnitch-demo.vercel.app
**Repo:** ~/creditsnitch (client-safe, nothing internal in it)

Presenter controls: `Shift + D` anywhere. `/?reset=1` resets everything.
Reset before you start. Open a second window on `/partner` and leave it behind
your main window; they stay in sync live.

---

## 0. Open (30 sec)

Land on the home page on your laptop. Let her read the headline.

> "Every credit app hands someone a report. This one tells them exactly what to
> do about it. That's your line, not mine."

Point at the QR code. **Have her scan it with her own phone.** She now holds the
product. Everything from here she does herself while you narrate on the laptop.

---

## 1. It starts with her question (90 sec)

Her phone: welcome screen, her photo, her name, her promise.

Tap through to the goal screen. Eleven goals, including **Build credit from
scratch**, **Build business credit**, and **I'm new to U.S. credit** — her real
programs, not a homebuyer-only tool.

> "The goal isn't a label. It changes which scores matter, how Readiness is
> measured and what her plan puts first."

Tap **Add income**. Enter anything.

> "A credit report never says what someone earns. Add it and debt-to-income
> becomes part of the plan. Credit Karma can't do that."

---

## 2. Connect and analyse (60 sec)

Tap through Connect. Let the sequence play: identity, authorization, then her
accounts streaming in one at a time, ending on the stage.

Point at the provider list before tapping.

> "SmartCredit does identity. Stripe does payments. GoHighLevel does messaging.
> Credit Snitch never sees a Social Security number, and never stores the report."

---

## 3. The moment that sells it (3 min)

Home screen. Maria is **Improving**, and it says why: an open collection.

> "Her numbers are better than her stage. One rule is holding the whole file
> back. That's the thing nobody else tells her."

Scroll. Three blockers, ranked, each tagged with one of her **Five Factors**.
The factor bar underneath is her own curriculum turned into a read of one person.

Go to **Plan**. Show the card: current → target, and a dashed chip that says what
that one action achieves.

**Press and hold the card** (hover on laptop). The track ghosts forward.

> "Do this, and here's where you land. Not a lecture. A forecast."

**Tap the first action done.** Watch it move Improving → Almost Qualified.
**Tap the second.** Qualified. The sweep fires, and the card slides up:
*Jane Smith is ready to help.*

Turn to the second window. Jane's pipeline already says **4 people are now
Qualified**, Maria among them. Nobody refreshed anything.

---

## 3b. Snitchy (2 min) — the new beat

Still on her phone, tap the Snitchy mark in the header.

The suggested questions are built from Maria's own file, not a generic list.
Tap **"Why is my collection blocking me?"**

The answer is in Angelique's voice, about Maria's file, and underneath it says
which lesson it came from.

> "It only answers from your material. Not from the internet, not from a model's
> opinion about credit. Yours."

**Hand her the phone and let her ask it something in her own words.** That is the
moment. If it does not know, it says so in plain words and logs the question.

Then on the laptop, Command Center → **Teach Snitchy**. Show the sources list,
then the unanswered questions.

> "Every question it could not answer lands here. That is the list of what to
> write down next, and it comes from your actual clients instead of guesswork."

Mention the team side: on any consumer profile there is a Snitchy panel that
drafts the monthly note in her voice and preps a call from the file.

---

## 4. Her teaching, her report (2 min)

**Report tab.** The whole report in plain words. Open Chase Freedom.
**Drag the balance slider.** Utilization, status and the Readiness track all
respond live.

> "Pay this much, land here. She can show a client that on a call."

Tap **Something looks wrong** → the dispute reasons → hands off to her dispute
portal. We don't rebuild what she already runs.

**Learn tab.** Not a video library. Her six programs, recommended by what's
actually on the report.

---

## 5. What we never keep (60 sec)

Settings → **How we handle your data**. Walk the table. Tap
**Show me** on "everything we hold about you".

> "That's the whole record. No report, no balances, no Social. It's a design
> decision, and it's why your security and insurance bill stays small."

---

## 6. The business (3 min)

Laptop → **/partner**. Pipeline, who to contact, who to leave alone, referral
link and QR that flips to the co-branded page. Switch the persona to **Alex ·
Lender** — same portal, different job.

**Referrals tab.** Point at the row with no consent: *Stage hidden, awaiting
their permission.* Then let her mark someone **Closed**.

> "That's the number that recruits your next fifty agents, and right now nobody
> is capturing it."

**Monthly update tab.** The report she sends by hand today, sent automatically.

Laptop → **/admin**. The Opportunity queue.

> "Tasha is Almost Qualified and buying in thirty days. Rosa is Qualified and
> thinking about it. Tasha is at the top. This ranks who needs you today, not
> who's closest."

Expand a row: the score breakdown and four next best actions, including what to
tell the partner.

**Rulebook.** Move the utilization slider and watch Maria change stage.

> "The engine does the arithmetic. These thresholds are your judgement. What
> Qualified means is your call, and it's in the software so it's applied the
> same way to everyone."

---

## 7. Close (60 sec)

Back to her phone, Progress tab. Milestones, and the share card.

> "Every client win goes out with your brand on it."

Then:

> "Your pricing principle is that someone's credit decides what you recommend,
> never what you charge. That's built in. Nothing here is gated because a file
> looks bad."

Ask for the decision.

---

## Say out loud, briefly

- Paid dispute letters need a credit repair compliance review before launch.
- Anything that pays a Realtor on a transaction needs a real estate settlement
  review first. That's why the rewards panel says "coming soon" and shows no
  number.
- SSNs are never stored, by design, not by policy.

## Answer if asked

- **"Is this real?"** The engine is real and tested. The credit data, the
  accounts and the people are invented.
- **"Where does Snitchy get its answers?"** Right now, from what Credit Snitch
  already publishes: the Five Factors, the programs, the guide, the rules the
  app runs on. In the real build she drops SOPs, scripts and call recordings
  into one folder and it indexes them. It only ever answers from her material.
- **"Could it say something wrong?"** It is instructed never to say anyone is
  approved or likely approved, never to give legal or tax advice, never to quote
  a price that is not in her material, and never to invent a policy. When it does
  not know, it says so instead of guessing.
- **"How long?"** Don't answer from the room. Scope phase one against what she
  picks in the next section.

## Decide in the room

1. Consumers first, partner referrals first, or both.
2. What Qualified means per goal. The Rulebook is the form to fill in.
3. How it's paid for: report, subscription, disputes, White Glove, partner tiers.
4. Which programs are in version one.
5. Is the partner portal "Connect X Network" or its own name? Connect X is
   currently her luxury off-market network, which is a different audience.
6. Does the dispute flow stay in her existing portal.
7. How far Spanish goes at launch.
8. Budget and a date.
