# Credit Snitch

## The concept, in one line

Every credit app hands someone a report. Credit Snitch tells them exactly what
to do about it, tracks whether they did it, and tells the right person when
they're ready.

**See it:** creditsnitch-demo.vercel.app

---

## What you looked at

**The app.** A consumer picks a goal, connects their credit, and gets a
Readiness stage, a ranked list of what's holding them back, and a plan in the
order it should be done. Each action shows what it achieves before they do it.
Your programs are the curriculum, recommended by what's actually on the report.
It speaks English and Spanish.

**Credit Snitch Partners.** A Realtor or loan officer sees who they referred,
who's progressing, who's ready and who to leave alone. Stages only, with the
consumer's recorded permission, and never a balance or a score. The monthly
update you send by hand today goes out on its own.

**The Command Center.** The whole business in one view, plus a queue that ranks
who deserves your team's time today. Not who is closest to their goal, which is
a different question.

**Snitchy.** Your playbook, answering. Clients ask it anything and get your
answer about their own file, with the lesson it came from named underneath. Your
team uses the same thing to draft the monthly note and prep a call. And every
question it could not answer is collected for you, which turns what you have not
written down yet into a list instead of a blind spot.

---

## Three decisions that are already yours

**Readiness is your rulebook, not a black box.** The engine does the
arithmetic. The thresholds that decide what Qualified means are a form you
fill in, per goal, and changing one re-evaluates everyone. Your judgement,
applied the same way to every client.

**The plan leads with the thing that's actually stopping them.** An open
collection caps a mortgage no matter how good the score looks. The app says so,
in those words, and puts it first.

**We read the report. We never keep it.** No Social Security numbers, no credit
files, no card numbers, no score history. Those live with the companies that
already specialise in protecting them. Credit Snitch holds a goal, a stage, a
plan and a consent record. That keeps your security, insurance and compliance
costs where a coaching business can carry them.

## What happens next

   the form.
2. Send us the SOPs, scripts and recordings you want Snitchy to learn first.
3. Decide who comes first at launch: consumers who find you, or partner
   referrals.
4. Pick the programs that ship in version one.
5. Give us a date and a budget range, and we'll scope phase one against them.

Two things need a specialist's sign-off before launch: paid dispute letters and
any arrangement that pays a Realtor on a transaction. We've built around both so
nothing has to be undone later.
